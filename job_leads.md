## Column guide

- **Source:** where the lead came from: a job board, a company careers page, a referral, or a person's name.
- **Role:** the job title, as the posting gives it.
- **Company:** the company name.
- **Location:** the location as the posting gives it, for example "Remote (US)". Mark it _(inferred)_ if the posting doesn't say.
- **Link:** the URL of the posting.
- **MatchReason:** the fit score and a one-line reason, scored against `job_search_profile.md`. For example: `High: TAM at a cybersecurity company, fully remote`.
- **Notes:** dated entries, newest first, separated by `<br>`. For example: `2026-11-05: Added by weekly run`.
- **OutreachStatus:** one of these values:
  - `Not started`: no outreach yet
  - `Drafted`: a draft is in `outreach/` waiting for your review
  - `Sent`: you sent a message (set only after you confirm)
  - `Replied`: someone responded
  - `In conversation`: a call or interview is scheduled or under way
  - `Applied`: you submitted an application
  - `Closed`: the lead ended (set only by you; say why in Notes)

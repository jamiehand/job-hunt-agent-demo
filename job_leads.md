# Job Leads

The running record of leads. Claude adds new leads and updates their status, and never deletes a lead or your notes. When a lead ends, set its status to `Closed` and note why.

**Statuses:** `New` → `Researching` → `Reaching out` → `Applied` → `In conversation` → `Offer` / `Closed`

**Fit:** `High` / `Medium` / `Low`, scored against `job_search_profile.md`

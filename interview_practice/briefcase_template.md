# Briefcase Template

A blank template for a short written proposal to bring to any interview. It shows how you'd approach the role, based on real research. Mention it near the beginning (for example, ask whether you can share your screen at some point) and let the interviewer choose when. See "Where the briefcase fits" in `interview_practice/question_bank.md` for when to use it.

**Public vs private:** this is the blank template. Copy it to `private/briefcase/<company>.md` (git-ignored) for each company. A company's version includes your research and what people told you, and you don't want the company to find it before you present it.

Keep the finished version to one or two pages.

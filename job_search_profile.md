# Job Search Profile

What a good-fit role looks like. Claude reads this before every search and lead review. **Must-haves** rule a lead out if they aren't met. **Preferences** only affect the fit score.

_Last updated: 2026-11-05_

## Target roles

| Priority | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Technical account manager (TAM) | Post-sale, technical, customer-facing |
| Primary | Customer success manager (CSM) | Adoption, retention, expansion |

- **Customer success engineer (CSE) roles** count as target roles, depending on how much coding they need:
  - **Config and troubleshooting** (configuring the product, troubleshooting, explaining APIs, reading logs, editing a simple script): a good fit. Score as usual. Note `Coding: config & troubleshooting`.
  - **APIs and scripting** (writing scripts to connect systems, working with APIs and JSON, some SQL): a role you can grow into. Score as usual. Note `Coding: APIs & scripting`.
  - **Production code** (fixing the product's bugs, shipping code changes): **Medium at most**. Note `Coding: production code`.
  - When the posting lists specific languages or tools, add them in parentheses, for example `Coding: APIs & scripting (Python, SQL)`, so you can see what to study for each role.
- **Seniority:** not set yet. Claude includes all levels until you set one.
- **Out of scope:** pre-sales roles (solutions engineer, sales engineer) and implementation-only roles, unless you add them above.

## Location and work setup

- **Must-have:** fully remote.
- **Country and time zone:** US, working Pacific time (PT) hours.
  - Roles that say US remote, or that name Pacific or West Coast hours, are fine.
  - Roles that require another region's working hours (for example, "East Coast preferred" or "Mountain or Central hours") score **Medium at most**. Note the hours requirement in the lead.
  - Roles open only outside the US are **Out**.
- **Relocation:** no.

Hybrid, on-site, and "remote" roles that require regular office days are ruled out.

## Fit scoring

- **High:** a target role, fully remote, and a target industry.
- **Medium:** a target role and fully remote, but in an adjacent industry.
- **Low:** meets the must-haves but is a weak match on role scope or industry.
- **Out:** breaks a must-have (for example, not fully remote).
- **Travel:** doesn't affect the score. Every lead notes its travel (for example, `Travel: up to 25%` or `Travel: not stated`) so you can decide. Travel for conferences, offsites and team gatherings is a plus.
- **Driving:** a role that requires driving (a driver's license, "reliable transportation", or living within driving distance of clients or sites) scores **Medium at most**, unless the posting makes clear it can be done by public transit or rideshare.
